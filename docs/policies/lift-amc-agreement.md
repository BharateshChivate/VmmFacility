# Lift Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)

**Contract type:** Comprehensive AMC with listed exclusions  
**Contract period:** 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31  
**Client:** VMM Facility Management (represented by the Facility Manager)  
**Vendor:** Example Elevator Services Pvt. Ltd. (the "Vendor")

## 1. Lifts covered

1.1 This contract covers the following lifts at the VMM premises:

| Lift ID | Type | Capacity | Floors served | Year installed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Passenger lift, Tower A | 8 persons / 544 kg | Basement to 12th floor | 2018 |
| L2 | Passenger lift, Tower B | 8 persons / 544 kg | Basement to 12th floor | 2018 |
| L3 | Service lift, Tower A | 13 persons / 884 kg | Basement to 12th floor | 2019 |

1.2 Any new lift added during the contract period is covered only after a written amendment.

## 2. Contract value and payment

2.1 The annual AMC fee is **₹1,80,000 plus 18% GST** for all three lifts (₹60,000 per lift per year before GST).

2.2 The fee is billed **quarterly in advance**: ₹45,000 plus GST of ₹8,100, a total of **₹53,100 per quarter**.

2.3 Invoices are raised on 1 April, 1 July, 1 October, and 1 January. VMM pays within **15 days** of receiving a correct invoice.

2.4 Charges for excluded parts and work (section 5) are billed separately, only after the Facility Manager approves a written quotation.

2.5 The fee is fixed for the contract period. Any increase on renewal is limited to 7% unless both parties agree otherwise in writing.

## 3. Preventive maintenance

3.1 The Vendor carries out **one preventive maintenance visit per lift every month**, on a date shared with the Facility Manager in advance.

3.2 Each visit follows the Vendor's standard checklist, including:

- Inspecting and lubricating the machine, guide rails, and door operators.
- Checking brakes, safety gear, overspeed governor, and limit switches.
- Checking door sensors, landing and car door locks, and levelling accuracy.
- Testing the alarm bell, intercom, emergency light, and automatic rescue device (ARD).
- Cleaning the machine room, car top, and lift pit.

3.3 The technician records the work in a service report, which the Facility Manager or the security supervisor signs. A copy is left at the security cabin.

3.4 Preventive visits are scheduled between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM to avoid peak usage. Only one lift per tower may be taken out of service at a time.

## 4. Breakdown service and response times

4.1 Breakdown calls are **unlimited and attended 24×7, including Sundays and public holidays**, at no extra charge.

4.2 Calls are raised on the Vendor helpline. Security staff record the time of the call and the complaint number in the lift register.

4.3 Response times:

| Situation | Vendor technician on site within |
|---|---|
| Passenger trapped in a lift (entrapment) | **30 minutes** |
| Lift stopped / not working, no one trapped | **4 hours** |
| Minor fault, lift still running (noise, jerks, button not lighting) | **Next working day** |

4.4 A breakdown should be fixed within **24 hours** of the technician arriving, unless parts must be ordered. If parts are needed, the Vendor gives the Facility Manager a written expected repair date.

## 5. What is covered and what is not

### 5.1 Parts and work covered at no extra cost

- All labour for preventive maintenance and breakdown repairs.
- Lubricants, grease, cotton waste, and cleaning materials.
- Contacts, relays, fuses, push buttons, indicator lamps and displays.
- Door rollers, door locks, door sensors (light curtain), and door operator belts.
- Brake linings, guide shoes, and governor rope tensioning.
- Car and landing call buttons, the intercom handset, and the alarm bell.
- Batteries of the automatic rescue device (ARD), once a year.

### 5.2 Exclusions (charged separately after quotation approval)

The following are **not covered** by the AMC fee:

- **Wire ropes (hoist ropes)** – replacement of ropes, which normally wear out after several years.
- **Controller board / main PCB** and variable-frequency drive (VFD) – repair or replacement.
- **Traction machine and motor** – rewinding, gearbox overhaul, or replacement of the machine.
- Car interior and finishes: cabin panels, flooring, false ceiling, mirrors, and fans.
- Landing and car door panels damaged by misuse, impact, or vandalism.
- Damage caused by water entering the lift pit or shaft (for example, flooding or leaks), fire, power surges, or unauthorised work by others.
- Civil, electrical supply, and building work (machine room repairs, power cabling up to the lift main switch, pit waterproofing).
- Modernisation or upgrades requested by VMM.

5.3 For any excluded item, the Vendor submits a written quotation. Work starts only after the Facility Manager approves it in writing, except when the lift must be made safe immediately.

5.4 Parts replaced under section 5.2 carry a **6-month warranty** from the date of fitting.

## 6. Uptime, penalties, and credits

6.1 The Vendor commits to each lift being available for at least **98% of the time each month**, excluding scheduled preventive maintenance and delays caused by excluded items awaiting VMM approval.

6.2 If the technician arrives later than the response time in section 4.3, VMM may deduct:

- **₹1,000 for each hour of delay** for an entrapment call.
- **₹500 for each hour of delay** for a lift stopped with no one trapped.

6.3 If a lift remains out of service for more than **48 hours** because of a covered fault, VMM may deduct **2% of that quarter's AMC fee for each additional day**, up to a maximum of 10% of the quarterly fee.

6.4 Deductions are adjusted against the next quarterly invoice. The Facility Manager records the deduction reason in the lift register.

## 7. Safety and emergency procedure

7.1 The lift machine room is kept locked. Keys are held by the security supervisor and the Vendor's technician only.

7.2 Housekeeping and security staff must **not** enter the machine room, lift shaft, car top, or lift pit.

7.3 In case of entrapment:

1. Security speaks to the trapped passengers on the intercom and keeps them calm.
2. Security calls the Vendor helpline immediately and records the call time.
3. Only the Vendor's technician, or a VMM staff member trained and certified in manual rescue by the Vendor, may carry out a manual rescue.
4. The lift stays out of service until the technician confirms it is safe.

7.4 The Vendor trains two VMM security staff in safe manual rescue once a year at no extra cost.

## 8. Statutory inspection and records

8.1 The Vendor assists with the **annual statutory lift inspection** by the government Lift Inspector, including preparing the lift and attending the inspection.

8.2 The inspection fee and licence renewal fee charged by the government are paid by VMM and are **not** part of the AMC fee.

8.3 The Vendor keeps a logbook for each lift with all preventive visits, breakdowns, parts replaced, and inspection results, and shares a copy with the Facility Manager every quarter.

## 10. Term, renewal, and termination

10.1 This contract runs from 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31.

10.2 The Vendor sends renewal terms at least **60 days** before expiry.

10.3 Either party may end the contract with **60 days' written notice**. VMM may end it with 15 days' notice if the response times in section 4.3 are missed more than three times in any quarter.

10.4 On termination, the Vendor hands over the logbooks, service reports, and machine room keys, and is paid only for the period served.
