# Housekeeping Working Policy

**Document ID:** VMM-POL-HK-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-04-01  
**Review date:** 2027-03-31  
**Owner:** Facility Manager, VMM Facility Management  
**Applies to:** All housekeeping staff, housekeeping supervisors, and contract cleaning agencies working at the VMM premises

> Sample document created for testing the VMM assistant. Names, figures, and terms are illustrative and should be replaced with the facility's approved policy before real use.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy sets the working rules for housekeeping at the VMM premises so that common areas stay clean, safe, and hygienic.

1.2 It defines shift timings, cleaning schedules, supplies and approvals, waste handling, safety rules, complaint handling, and how housekeeping costs are recorded in the VMM Facility Management app.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy covers all common areas: entrance lobby, corridors, staircases, lift cars and lift lobbies, basement and parking, terrace, garden walkways, security cabin, clubhouse, gym, common washrooms, and the garbage collection area.

2.2 It does not cover the inside of individual flats or offices, unless a separate paid service request is approved by the Facility Manager.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

3.1 **Facility Manager (FM):** approves the cleaning schedule, staffing plan, supply purchases above the supervisor's limit, and all housekeeping payments. Records every housekeeping cost in the VMM app.

3.2 **Housekeeping Supervisor:** assigns daily duties, checks attendance, inspects work, keeps the supplies register, raises purchase requests, and reports issues to the FM.

3.3 **Housekeeping staff:** carry out assigned cleaning tasks, use the correct chemicals and protective equipment, segregate waste, and report damage, leaks, or safety hazards to the supervisor immediately.

3.4 **Contract cleaning agency (if engaged):** provides trained staff, replacements for absent staff, uniforms, and statutory compliance (wages, PF, ESI) for its employees.

## 4. Working hours and staffing

4.1 Housekeeping runs in two shifts, Monday to Sunday:

| Shift | Timing | Minimum staff |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | 6:00 AM – 2:00 PM | 4 staff + 1 supervisor |
| Evening | 2:00 PM – 10:00 PM | 2 staff |

4.2 Each staff member works a maximum of 8 hours per shift and 48 hours per week, with one weekly off on a rotating roster.

4.3 Staff must sign in and sign out in the attendance register at the security cabin. Late arrival of more than 15 minutes is recorded by the supervisor.

4.4 Leave must be requested at least 2 days in advance, except in emergencies. The supervisor or agency must arrange a replacement so that the minimum staffing in 4.1 is maintained.

4.5 Overtime is allowed only with the FM's written approval and is paid at twice the normal hourly rate.

## 5. Cleaning schedule

### 5.1 Daily tasks

- Sweep and wet-mop the entrance lobby, corridors, and lift lobbies (morning and evening).
- Clean lift cars: floor, mirror, buttons, and steel panels (twice daily). Use only a dry or lightly damp cloth on lift buttons and panels; never spray liquid directly on them.
- Clean and disinfect common washrooms (three times daily) and update the washroom cleaning checklist on the door.
- Empty dustbins in common areas and replace bin liners.
- Sweep staircases and wipe handrails.
- Clean the security cabin and the clubhouse reception.

### 5.2 Weekly tasks

- Scrub the basement and parking floor (every Sunday).
- Clean glass doors and lobby windows (every Wednesday).
- Clean the terrace and remove debris from terrace drains (every Saturday).
- Wash the garbage collection area and bins with disinfectant (every Monday and Thursday).

### 5.3 Monthly tasks

- Deep clean staircases, including corners and skirting (first week of the month).
- Clean ceiling fans, light fittings, and cobwebs in common areas (second week).
- Polish steel surfaces in lift lobbies and handrails (third week).
- Clean overhead and underground water tank areas from outside (fourth week). Internal tank cleaning is done by a specialised vendor every 6 months and is not a housekeeping task.

### 5.4 Special cleaning

- After heavy rain, clear water from the basement and parking ramp within 2 hours.
- After events in the clubhouse, complete cleaning before 10:00 AM the next day.
- After any water leak, clean the area only after the leak source has been isolated by the maintenance team.

## 6. Supplies, chemicals, and equipment

6.1 Only approved cleaning chemicals may be used: floor cleaner, toilet cleaner, glass cleaner, disinfectant, and steel polish from the approved supplies list kept by the supervisor.

6.2 Acid-based cleaners must not be used on marble, granite, or lift steel panels.

6.3 The supervisor keeps a supplies register with stock received, stock issued, and balance. Stock is checked every Monday.

6.4 **Purchase approvals:**

| Purchase value (per request) | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹2,000 | Housekeeping Supervisor |
| ₹2,001 – ₹10,000 | Facility Manager |
| Above ₹10,000 | Facility Manager, with three written quotations |

6.5 Equipment (mops, buckets, scrubbing machine, vacuum cleaner) must be cleaned and stored in the housekeeping store after each shift. Faulty equipment is reported to the supervisor the same day.

## 7. Waste management

7.1 Waste is segregated at source into three streams:

| Bin colour | Type | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Wet waste | Food waste, garden waste |
| Blue | Dry waste | Paper, plastic, cardboard, metal |
| Red | Hazardous / sanitary | Sanitary waste, used batteries, bulbs, chemical containers |

7.2 Mixed waste is not handed over to the municipal collector. Staff must re-segregate it and report repeat offenders to the FM.

7.3 Municipal waste collection is at 7:30 AM daily. Waste must be ready at the collection point by 7:15 AM.

7.4 E-waste and old furniture are collected separately on request and recorded by the FM.

## 8. Health and safety

8.1 Staff must wear gloves and closed footwear for all cleaning tasks, and a mask and goggles when using chemicals or cleaning washrooms.

8.2 A "Wet Floor" sign must be placed before mopping any walkway, lobby, or staircase, and removed only when the floor is dry.

8.3 Staff must not enter the lift machine room, lift shaft, or lift pit for any reason. These areas are handled only by the lift AMC vendor (see the Lift AMC agreement, VMM-AMC-LIFT-2026).

8.4 Staff must not clean electrical panels, switches, or DG set areas.

8.5 Any injury, however minor, is reported to the supervisor immediately and recorded in the incident register. The first aid box is kept in the security cabin.

## 9. Uniform and conduct

9.1 Staff wear the issued uniform and ID card at all times on duty.

9.2 Staff must be polite to residents and visitors, must not accept tips, and must not enter any flat or office without a supervisor's instruction.

9.3 Mobile phones may be used only during breaks, except for work communication.

## 10. Complaints and service levels

10.1 Residents raise housekeeping complaints at the security cabin or with the FM.

10.2 Response times:

| Complaint type | Response time |
|---|---|
| Spillage, vomit, or blocked washroom in a common area | Within 30 minutes |
| Missed daily cleaning task | Same shift |
| General cleaning request | Within 24 hours |

10.3 The supervisor closes each complaint in the complaint register with the time it was resolved.

## 11. Inspections and quality

11.1 The supervisor inspects all common areas once per shift using the daily checklist.

11.2 The FM carries out a surprise inspection at least once a week and records the score (out of 10) for each area.

11.3 If a contract agency scores below 7 out of 10 for two consecutive weeks, the FM may deduct up to 5% of that month's agency invoice, as allowed in the agency contract.

## 12. Recording housekeeping costs in the VMM app

12.1 Every housekeeping cost is recorded as an expense in the VMM Facility Management app on the day the bill or invoice is received.

12.2 Use these categories:

| Cost | Category in the VMM app |
|---|---|
| Contract cleaning agency invoice | Housekeeping |
| Overtime payments for housekeeping staff | Housekeeping |
| Cleaning chemicals, bin liners, mops, brushes | Supplies |
| Scrubbing machine or vacuum cleaner repair | Maintenance & Repairs |
| Specialised water tank cleaning | Other |

12.3 The description must include what was bought or paid for and the period or invoice number, for example "Housekeeping agency invoice – October 2026" or "Cleaning supplies restock – floor cleaner and bin liners".

12.4 Mark the expense as **Unpaid** when the bill is received and update it to **Paid** after payment.

12.5 The monthly housekeeping budget is ₹45,000 (agency charges and overtime) plus ₹6,000 for supplies. The FM reviews actual spending against this budget in the first week of each month.

## 13. Policy review

13.1 This policy is reviewed every year before the review date, or earlier if staffing, the agency contract, or local regulations change.

13.2 Changes are approved by the Facility Manager and shared with all housekeeping staff.
